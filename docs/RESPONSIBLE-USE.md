# Responsible use

Use NEXUS to investigate attributable public observations, not to identify attackers from hosting locations. Routing changes and outages have many legitimate causes. Candidate labels are not confirmed incidents. Missing coverage does not establish safety.

Only passive public feeds are allowed in NEXUS LIVE. Do not probe, scan or resolve observed targets. Keep raw personal data out of public outputs and use aggregate counts with explicit units, timestamps, scope and attribution.

Public access is not a republication license. IODA and certificate collection remain excluded while rights and capacity are unresolved. Respect RIPE's conditions and the mapping dataset's license. No paid agents are authorized; deployed workers remain inactive at $0. A human reviews and merges each change.
