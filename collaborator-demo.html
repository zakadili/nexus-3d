01 — VISION

Know what's exposed.
Understand the risk. Verify the fix.

NEXUS is building accessible, evidence-backed exposure intelligence for organizations without large security teams.

◎
Observe

Collect public vulnerability and threat intelligence with timestamps, attribution, and coverage limitations.

⬡
Verify

Confirm assets and findings within an authorized customer workspace before calling something exposed.

✓

The differentiator

Not another world map claiming every event is an attack. NEXUS separates what a source observed, what was enriched, and what a customer has actually verified.
