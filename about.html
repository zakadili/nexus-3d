ABOUT NEXUS SENTINEL

Understand the evidence.

A public research project for exploring published IP indicators and investigating exploited vulnerabilities. Maintained by zakadili. NEXUS is an independent project, not an official service of its data providers.

Choose a task

  • Explore countries: compare listed IP infrastructure within collected feeds.
  • Browse exploited vulnerabilities: search CISA's catalog, then open the CVE workbench.
  • Try a CVE investigation: inspect Log4Shell with attributed source evidence.
  • Look up an indicator: search locally within explicitly covered, redistributable data.
  • Plan an assessment: use typed CVEs and package identifiers while keeping reports in your own internal tools.

Privacy and data flow

This site contains no advertising or analytics scripts. Hosting and API providers receive normal request information, such as IP address and browser headers, when your browser contacts them. Their own privacy policies apply.

Understand live signals

Live signals are independent observations of Internet routing, separate from the six-hour snapshot map. They do not represent a complete feed of cyberattacks. A country marker describes ASN registration, not an attacker. Open a live item for its source, time, confidence and temporary evidence link. Pause updates whenever you need to read; hidden tabs disconnect.

Unavailable, disabled and permission-blocked sources produce no replacement activity. IODA and certificate collection are not enabled. Read the live-source definitions, credits and exclusions.

Live requests go to the NEXUS service only after you open the panel. Its new signal buffer keeps short aggregates and opaque provenance digests in memory; it does not publish raw peer addresses or domains. Providers still receive normal connection metadata. Existing research features and legacy feeds have their own data flows described above.

A short glossary

CVE
A public identifier for a documented vulnerability.
KEV
CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Listing confirms exploitation has been observed somewhere; it does not verify your assets are affected.
EPSS
FIRST's model estimate of exploitation activity within the next 30 days. It is a probability, not a severity rating or a target-specific verdict.
IOC
Indicator of compromise: an address, domain, hash or other value used in investigation. Listing and coverage require context.
ASN
Autonomous System Number: a routing-network identifier. A provider hosting listed IPs is not necessarily malicious.
SBOM
Software bill of materials: a list of software components and versions.

Methods, accessibility and feedback

Read the source directory and measurement limits. The map has country-search and ranked-list alternatives. Auto-rotation starts paused; reduced-motion preferences are respected. Use Tab to move between controls and Escape to close country search.

Report a problem or suggest an improvement. Include the page, browser, screen size and steps to reproduce. Avoid posting private inventories or credentials in public issues.
