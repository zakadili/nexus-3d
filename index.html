WHAT IS THIS DATA?

Observations come from the SANS Internet Storm Center / DShield volunteer sensor network. Country is inferred from the reported source IP's network geolocation (ipwho.is). These observations can include benign research scanners and false positives. Country is not attacker attribution. Port figures are independent sensor summaries, not destinations paired with individual source IPs. Counters cover the displayed sampled feed—not global totals. Data is cached to respect upstream services. Read the methodology ↗
