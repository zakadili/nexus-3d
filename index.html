WHAT IS THIS DATA?

Listed IP intelligence comes from blocklist.de, IPsum, DShield, and abuse.ch, with approximate country assignment from DB-IP Lite. Country of IP infrastructure is not attacker attribution. These lists have different meanings and may contain false positives or benign research scanners. CISA KEV, FIRST EPSS, and ENISA sector data are separate datasets, not directly comparable.
